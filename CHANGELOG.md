# Changelog

## 0.10.0 — 2026-11-05

- Miller: two giant waves instead of five, the second one already coming as the first passes (like the film), and
  a rumble that grows as a crest gets close.
- Time dilation on Miller (1 hour = 7 years outside), applied when you return to the Space Center or Tracking
  Station. New setting `TimeDilation` (0 turns it off).
- Real Scale: new setting `RealScale` makes the whole Gargantua system 10x bigger; on automatically with RSS/KSRSS
  (replaces the old 113x Gargantua mass patch for RSS + DistanceFactor 0).
- Mann: aurora from the pulsar wind (EVE).
- Custom science results (crew report, EVA report, surface sample) in English and Brazilian Portuguese.
- Loading screens with in-game shots of the system.
- Optional: Firefly reentry colours, Scatterer sun flares for Pantagruel and the pulsar, Kerbal Konstructs Plan B
  base on Edmunds.

## 0.9.0 — 2026-10-17 (first public beta)

- Gargantua: Singularity black hole with a lensed, tilted accretion disk.
- Miller: shallow ocean world with Scatterer ocean, overcast volumetric clouds and five giant tidal waves that sweep
  the planet. The waves lift and carry floating vessels and Kerbals with buoyancy and water drag, and drop time
  warp with a warning when a crest is 33 km away.
- Mann: glacier world with 8K color map, 16-bit heightmap, frozen cloud sheets and Parallax rocks.
- Edmunds and Pantagruel: rocky desert world around a main-sequence star, with Parallax rocks.
- Neutron star: glowing pulsar with animated jets that does not steal scene lighting.
- Optional wormhole Kerbol (Jool / Sarnus / Saturn) ↔ Gargantua.
- Settings: Wormholes, DistanceFactor, DisableSkybox.
- Compatibility patches: Principia, RSS, KSRSS, OPM, Scatterer, EVE, Parallax Continued, SigmaReplacements-SkyBox.
- English and Brazilian Portuguese localization.
